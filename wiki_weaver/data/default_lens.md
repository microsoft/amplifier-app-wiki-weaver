# Lens

## Purpose
A general-purpose memory of the sources dropped into this wiki, so an agent can orient
quickly and follow any thread back to the source.

## What matters
Decisions and how they changed; commitments and who made them; open questions; recurring
themes across sources. Routine chatter and pleasantries matter little.

## People
Anyone named in the sources. Record who they are and what they work on, as the sources say.

## Question shapes
"What is the state of X?" "Who is working on Y?" "What was decided about Z, and when?"
"What changed since <date>?"

## Page types
- topics - one page per recurring subject
  Current state covers: what it is, where it stands, what is open.
- people - one page per person who recurs
  Current state covers: what they work on now, what they have committed to.
- log - dated pages for events that matter on their own
  Current state covers: what happened and what came of it.
